# Trader Performance vs Market Sentiment Analysis

## 📌 Objective
Analyze how market sentiment (Fear/Greed) impacts trader behavior and performance.

---

## 📊 Datasets
- Bitcoin Fear/Greed Index
- Historical Trader Data (Hyperliquid)

---

## ⚙️ Methodology
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Date alignment and merging
- Feature engineering (PnL, win rate, trade size, activity)
- Sentiment-based analysis with visualizations
- Bonus: Predictive modeling using Random Forest

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Traders achieve highest profitability during Extreme Greed
- Trading activity is highest during Fear periods
- High activity does not always lead to higher profits
- Market sentiment significantly influences trading outcomes

---

## 💡 Strategy Recommendations
- Increase exposure during strong bullish sentiment (Extreme Greed)
- Avoid overtrading during Fear periods

---

## 🤖 Bonus: Predictive Model
A Random Forest model was used to predict trade profitability.

- Accuracy: 74%
- Key features: trade size, sentiment, trade side, and fees
